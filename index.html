    Curdography 101

    Curds squeak because the protein is still young and tight. Give it two days and the squeak is gone — at that point you're just eating cheese. Fresh is the whole game. Warm ones squeak louder than cold ones, and a microwave brings a dead curd about halfway back. This map tracks where the squeak still lives.
